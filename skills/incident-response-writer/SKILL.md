# Incident Response Writer

## Process
1. Use only validated facts and clearly marked inferences.
2. Tailor detail to the audience: technical, operational, or executive.
3. Record impact, timeline, cause, remediation, validation, and remaining risks.
4. Avoid blame and focus on systems, controls, and process improvement.
5. Preserve source references and change/ticket identifiers.

## Output Options
- Technical ticket update
- Executive summary
- Resolution note
- Post-incident report

## Rules
- Do not name individuals as the cause of an incident.
- Do not expose secrets or unnecessary personal data.
- State uncertainty and open actions transparently.
